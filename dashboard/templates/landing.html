System Concept

What We're Building

A multi-tier connected ecosystem providing real-time biometric tracking, hazardous gas detection, and autonomous rover teleoperation.

monitor_heart
[01]

Wearable Safety Band

Continuously monitors worker Blood Oxygen (SpO₂) and pulse rate via MAX30100 oximetry; transmits biometric telemetry to the nearest checkpoint over ESP-NOW.

sensors
[02]

Smart Checkpoint Box

Fixed junction units deployed throughout tunnels monitoring combustible gases (MQ5), CO (MQ7), toxic air (MQ135), temperature & humidity (DHT11), PIR motion (HW-416-B), and acoustic spikes (HW-484).

notifications_active
[03]

Early Pre-Alert Information

Automatically detects threshold breaches in vitals or gas levels, alerting the surface command station before catastrophic incidents escalate.

smart_toy
[04]

Rescue Support Rover

Reconnaissance rover deployed into hazardous or collapsed tunnels with live OV2640 optical visual transmission and sub-millisecond teleoperation steering.

Architecture & Specs

Technical Approach

Hardware pin specifications, distributed mesh networking, and implementation methodology.

watch

Wearable Safety Band

  • MAX30100 Pulse Oximeter & Heart Rate
  • ESP32-WROOM Short-Range Unit
  • 3.7V Li-Ion Power Battery Pack
sensors

Checkpoint Sensors (ESP32 #4)

  • MQ5 Combustible Gas (GPIO 33)
  • MQ7 Carbon Monoxide (GPIO 34)
  • MQ135 Air Quality (GPIO 35)
  • HW-416-B PIR Motion (GPIO 26)
  • HW-484 Sound Spike (GPIO 27)
  • DHT11 Temp & Humidity (GPIO 15)
smart_toy

Recon Rover Platform

  • ESP32-CAM (OV2640 5 FPS Stream)
  • L298N / Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver
  • Sub-millisecond UDP Broadcast Steering
  • Failsafe 2.0s Auto-Stop Timeout

format_list_numbered Implementation Methodology

01

Sensing

Safety band and checkpoint sensors collect live environmental parameters.

02

Collection

Local ESP32 processors aggregate and package analog/digital inputs.

03

ESP-NOW

Direct peer-to-peer radio broadcast to the nearest checkpoint node.

04

Mesh Relay

Multi-hop forwarding across checkpoints if outside direct station range.

05

Wi-Fi Gateway

Gateway checkpoint pushes JSON payload to Central Station HTTP REST.

06

Station Processing

Rolling telemetry queues update trend graphs and evaluate safety limits.

07

Alert & Action

Immediate audio-visual alert streams triggered upon threshold breaches.

08

Rover Deployment

Live optical teleoperation deployed for zero-risk pre-rescue inspection.

Practicality & Costs

Feasibility & Viability

Cost-effective components, modular assembly, and deployment readiness for underground coal mines.

check_circle Hardware Feasibility

  • done Readily available industrial gas & biometric sensors (MQ-series, MAX30100, DHT11).
  • done Low-power dual-core ESP32 microcontrollers with integrated Wi-Fi and ESP-NOW radio.
  • done Modular checkpoint junction box design with soldered pin stability.
  • done Standard PC-based central monitoring station with zero specialized hardware requirements.

check_circle Deployment Feasibility

  • done Rapid deployment of multiple checkpoints across active tunnel extraction zones.
  • done Lightweight wearable safety band worn on miners' wrists throughout work shifts.
  • done Remote visual inspection via rover avoiding hazardous human entry.
  • done Checkpoint-to-checkpoint mesh relaying without needing extensive underground cabling.
attach_money Cost & Constraint Optimization Matrix
Challenge / Underground Constraint Low-Cost Optimized Solution
Underground network cabling limitations ESP-NOW wireless mesh relay between checkpoints
Wi-Fi unavailable across deep shafts Only the station gateway checkpoint requires Wi-Fi connectivity
Flammable / toxic gas accumulation Cost-effective MQ5, MQ7, and MQ135 multi-gas sensing suite
Sudden structural collapse or explosion sound HW-484 acoustic sound spike monitoring
Worker hypoxia / biometric emergencies MAX30100 optical pulse oximetry wearable bands

troubleshoot Identified Challenges & Future Enhancements

warning Challenge: Optical Visibility

Normal camera feed visibility may degrade in heavy smoke or coal dust haze.

Future Enhancement: Integrate lightweight thermal infrared camera modules for worker heat signature detection.
wifi_off Challenge: Deep Distance

Very deep mines exceeding standard radio distances.

Future Enhancement: Sub-GHz LoRa relay repeaters for multi-kilometer subterranean data transmission.
